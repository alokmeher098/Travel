Questions people ask

Who plans my trip?

Verified travel agencies plan and run every trip on Envelora. We check the agencies, connect you with them, and run the app that guides you during the trip.

Will I really not know where I’m going on a Sealed trip?

No. You get packing hints like “carry a warm jacket” a few days before, and the envelope tells you the destination on the day you leave.

Why are surprise trips only in my own state?

Sealed trips travel by road, so they stay within the state you start from. That keeps journey times comfortable.

Can I go somewhere that isn’t listed?

Yes. Name any place in the form and we’ll send packages from agencies that cover it within 24 hours.

What does the trip companion cost?

₹200 per trip, whether you book a package on Envelora or plan the trip yourself.

How do payment and cancellations work?

You pay once you approve the plan, by UPI or a payment link. The written cancellation and refund policy is shared before you pay.

Can I gift a trip to someone?

Yes. Mention it in the form and we’ll add a gift card to the envelope.
