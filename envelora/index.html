How it works

From a letter to a shared journey in three steps

You can plan without an account. You only need to log in when you're ready to bring everyone together.

STEP 1

Write the letter

Pick a destination and your dates, add each traveller, then fill in every day: times, places, cab pickups, driver details, hotel check-in and the night halt.

Day plan · Packing · Place guide
STEP 2

Seal it into a Clan

Pay once for the whole group and get a private invite link. Share it on WhatsApp, SMS or email; each person joins with their phone number or email.

STEP 3

Travel together

The Clan chat pins a Today card at the top with what's next, who's driving and what you owe. Set reminders, alert everyone, share photos and play games on the way.

Questions

Good to know

Do I need an account to plan?

No. You can plan the whole trip as a guest. When you log in, the trip you drafted moves into your account automatically.

How is the Clan price worked out?

Clans are free during the beta. After the beta: ₹39 × number of travellers × number of trip days. Five friends on a four-day trip pay ₹780 in total, about ₹156 each.

What if more people want to join?

When the clan is full, new people see "This clan is full. Ask your clan leader to increase capacity." The leader or a co-leader can add one or two spots at a time.

Who can change the itinerary?

Only the clan leader edits the plan. Everyone else can see it, and anyone can add expenses.

Which places have guides?

All of them. Goa, Manali, Jaipur, Udaipur, Rishikesh, Varanasi, Ooty and Shimla have detailed guides. Every other city, town and getaway (about 1,400 of them, across all 36 states and union territories) gets a quick guide with its state's food, shopping, activities and tips, plus nearby places and Google Maps links.
