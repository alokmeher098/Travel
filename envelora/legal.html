Privacy Policy

1. What we collect

2. Consent and your choices

We process your data on the basis of your consent, which you give when you create an account or turn on a feature. You can withdraw consent at any time by turning a feature off or deleting your account; this does not affect processing already done.

3. Where your data is stored

Your account, trips, clans, chat, tickets and shared locations are stored with our database provider Supabase on servers in Mumbai, India, and a copy is kept on your phone so the app works offline. Data sent to the content services listed below may be processed outside India by those providers.

4. Who we share it with

  • Your clan: members see what you post in that clan.
  • Service providers who work for us: Supabase (database and login, servers in Mumbai), our SMS provider for mobile login codes, our email provider for email login codes, and GitHub Pages, which hosts the website. They may use your data only to provide their service to us.
  • Content services that receive a place name or map position (not your name or contact): Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons (stories and photos), OpenStreetMap (map tiles and nearby places), Open-Meteo (weather), BigDataCloud (place name for your location) and Google Fonts.
  • Links you choose to open: Google Maps, Google Calendar and WhatsApp receive what you share when you tap those links.
  • Authorities: only when the law requires it.

We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account and trip data: until you delete it or your account.
  • Deleted or cancelled clans: removed with their chat and tickets.
  • Accounts unused for 3 years may be deleted after we notify you 48 hours in advance.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access a summary of your data, correct or update it, erase it, nominate someone to act for you in case of death or incapacity, and raise a grievance. Write to our Grievance Officer (see Contact). We respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

7. Children

You must be 18 or older to create an account. Travellers under 18 may be added to a trip only by a parent or guardian who consents on their behalf.

8. Security

We use one-time-code login, a security check against bots, encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls. If a breach affects your data, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

9. Changes

We will show a notice in the app before important changes take effect.

3. Free beta

  • Envelora is in a free beta. All features, including Clans, are free and no payment details are asked for.
  • Beta features may change, break or be removed, and data may occasionally be lost. Keep your own copies of important tickets and documents.

4. Clans

The leader manages the clan, its capacity and co-leaders, and may remove members or cancel the clan. Leaders and co-leaders can upload tickets for the clan.

5. Your content

You own what you post. You give us permission to store and show it to the people you share it with, only to run Envelora. Do not post anything unlawful, abusive, misleading, or that infringes someone else's rights. We may remove such content and suspend accounts that break these terms.

6. Information in the app

Place guides, stories, photos, weather and nearby places come partly from third parties such as Wikipedia and may be incomplete or out of date. Always check timings, permits, prices, weather and road conditions with official sources. Expense splits are calculations only; members settle money between themselves. The tickets feature only stores copies; it does not issue or validate tickets.

7. Safety features

SOS, location sharing and arrival alerts work on a best-effort basis and depend on your phone, network and permissions. Envelora is not an emergency service. In an emergency, call 112.

8. Liability

9. Ending the service
